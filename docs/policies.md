# Foodly Customer Policies

_Last updated: 2026-08-26_

This document is the knowledge base for Foodly's customer support chatbot. It covers ordering, delivery, payments, cancellations, refunds, accounts, promotions, conduct, group and business ordering, subscriptions, accessibility, rewards, and support operations. Each section is self-contained so it can be chunked, embedded, or quoted directly by a chatbot without losing context. A quick-answer FAQ is included at the end for direct Q&A grounding.

---

## 1. Ordering

- Customers can order from any restaurant partner shown as **"Open"** in the app. Restaurants marked "Closed" or "Unavailable" cannot be ordered from.
- An order is confirmed once the restaurant accepts it, typically within 2 minutes of submission. Orders not accepted within 5 minutes are automatically canceled and refunded in full.
- Minimum order values, if any, are set per restaurant and shown before checkout.
- Customers may add special instructions (e.g. "no onions," "leave at door") in the notes field. Restaurants and couriers make reasonable efforts to honor them but cannot guarantee compliance for allergy-critical requests — see Section 8 (Food Safety & Allergies).
- Once a restaurant begins preparing an order, it can no longer be modified. To change an order, cancel it (if eligible, see Section 4) and place a new one.
- Scheduled orders ("order for later") can be placed up to 7 days in advance for restaurants that support it; the requested delivery window is a target, not a guarantee, and is subject to the same delay conditions as immediate orders.
- Reordering a past order pulls current prices and availability — items that have been discontinued or price-changed are flagged before checkout so nothing is charged silently.
- Orders placed through a third-party integration (e.g. a restaurant's own website using Foodly for fulfillment) are still covered by this policy document; whichever surface the order originated from, refunds and support are still handled through the Foodly app or foodly.com support channels.

## 2. Pricing & Payments

- Menu prices are set by the restaurant and may differ slightly from in-store prices.
- Total cost includes: item subtotal, delivery fee, service fee, applicable taxes, and optional tip.
- Accepted payment methods: credit/debit cards, digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and Foodly account credit. Cash on delivery is available only in select cities and is marked at checkout.
- Payment is authorized at checkout and charged once the order is confirmed by the restaurant. If an order is canceled before confirmation, the authorization is released (funds typically reappear within 3–5 business days, timing depends on the customer's bank).
- Prices, fees, and delivery ranges can change based on demand (surge pricing) during peak times; the final price is always shown before payment is confirmed.
- Split payments (dividing one order's cost across two or more cards or wallets) are supported at checkout on a subset of orders; group order payment splitting is covered separately in Section 14.
- Currency is always shown in the customer's local currency for the city they're ordering in; Foodly does not charge foreign transaction fees, though the customer's card issuer might.
- If a duplicate charge appears (e.g. from a retried payment after a network error), it resolves automatically within 24 hours; if it does not, support can confirm and reverse it manually.

## 3. Delivery

- Delivery availability and estimated time are shown before checkout and are based on courier availability, distance, and restaurant prep time.
- Average delivery time is 25–40 minutes; this is an estimate, not a guarantee.
- Customers can track their order in real time from the "Order Status" screen, including courier location once the order is picked up.
- Couriers will attempt delivery at the address provided. If a customer is unreachable for more than 10 minutes after the courier arrives, the order may be marked as delivered or returned, at the courier's discretion, without a refund.
- Contactless delivery ("leave at door") can be selected at checkout for eligible orders.
- Delivery areas are limited to each restaurant's active radius, shown automatically based on the customer's delivery address.
- Delivery instructions (gate codes, apartment/unit numbers, building notes) should be added to the address profile in Settings rather than the order notes, so couriers see them before they arrive rather than after.
- Multi-stop delivery batching (a courier carrying more than one active order at once) may add a few minutes to the estimate during peak hours; this is factored into the estimated time shown at checkout, not an unexpected delay.
- Delivery to secure buildings, campuses, or venues that require lobby handoff is supported where the building allows courier access; if access is denied on arrival, the order follows the same unreachable-customer handling described above.

## 4. Cancellations

- **Before restaurant acceptance:** Orders can be canceled free of charge from the app.
- **After restaurant acceptance, before preparation starts:** Orders can usually still be canceled with a full refund; a short grace-period window is shown in the app.
- **After preparation has started:** Orders generally cannot be canceled, since the restaurant has already committed ingredients and labor. Refunds at this stage are handled case-by-case (see Section 5).
- **After the order has been picked up by a courier:** Cancellation is not possible. Contact support if there is an issue with the delivery itself.
- Scheduled orders can be canceled or rescheduled free of charge any time up until 30 minutes before the requested delivery window; inside that window, the standard cancellation stages above apply.
- Canceling repeatedly right at the preparation-start boundary may flag an account for manual review, since it can resemble an attempt to get free food preparation without payment.

## 5. Refunds & Order Issues

Foodly issues refunds or credits for the following, upon request within **48 hours** of delivery:

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Order never arrived | Full refund |
| Missing item(s) | Refund or credit for missing item(s) |
| Wrong item(s) delivered | Refund or credit for incorrect item(s) |
| Item(s) arrived damaged or spilled | Refund or credit for affected item(s) |
| Order significantly late (customer no longer wants it) | Reviewed case-by-case; may qualify for partial/full refund |
| Poor food quality (cold, undercooked, etc.) | Reviewed case-by-case; may qualify for credit |

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method or as Foodly account credit, customer's choice where available.
- Refund processing takes 5–10 business days to appear on a statement, depending on the bank.
- Repeated refund requests on an account may trigger manual review to prevent abuse.
- Delivery fees and tips are refunded only when the order was not delivered at all, or at support's discretion for severe service failures.
- A photo of the received order is not required to request a refund, but including one (via the in-app "Report an issue" flow) speeds up automatic approval for common issues like missing or wrong items.
- Partial refunds (for a subset of items in a multi-item order) are itemized in the receipt so it's clear exactly what was and wasn't refunded.
- Refund requests made after the 48-hour window are still reviewed by support on a case-by-case basis but are not guaranteed, since ingredient and preparation records age out on the restaurant side.

## 6. Accounts

- One Foodly account per person; accounts are non-transferable.
- Customers must be 18+ to create an account and place orders (age verification may be required for alcohol purchases).
- Account email and phone number can be updated in Settings. Payment methods can be added/removed at any time.
- Accounts inactive for 24+ months may have saved payment methods removed for security; the account itself is not deleted.
- Customers can request full account deletion and data removal at any time via Settings → Privacy → Delete Account, or by contacting support. This is processed within 30 days.
- Multiple delivery addresses (home, work, other) can be saved per account, with one marked as default; the correct address is confirmed at checkout every time.
- Signing in on a new device does not require re-verifying payment methods already saved to the account, but does require the usual login authentication (password, passcode, or social sign-in, depending on how the account was created).
- Merging two accounts (e.g. after a customer accidentally created a second one) is not self-service; support can transfer order history and saved credit on request after identity verification.

## 7. Promotions, Discounts & Referrals

- Promo codes are single-use per account unless stated otherwise, cannot be combined unless explicitly allowed, and may have minimum order values or restaurant restrictions.
- Foodly reserves the right to cancel a promotion, or revoke a discount retroactively, in cases of suspected fraud or abuse (e.g., creating multiple accounts to claim a first-order discount repeatedly).
- Referral rewards are credited only after the referred user completes their first qualifying order.
- Free delivery subscriptions (if offered) renew automatically until canceled and are billed per the plan's stated terms shown at signup. See Section 16 (Foodly Plus Membership) for full subscription details.
- Promotional credit expires according to the date shown when it was issued (typically 30 or 90 days); expired credit cannot be reinstated.
- Restaurant-funded promotions (discounts a restaurant runs on its own menu) are honored the same way as Foodly-funded ones from the customer's perspective, though which party ultimately covers the discount does not change refund or cancellation eligibility.

## 8. Food Safety & Allergies

- Foodly is a delivery platform; food is prepared by independent restaurant partners, who are responsible for ingredient accuracy and safe food handling.
- Customers with severe allergies should contact the restaurant directly (via in-app chat, where available) before ordering and should not rely solely on menu descriptions or special-instruction notes.
- Foodly cannot guarantee any dish is allergen-free, even if requested. When in doubt, the safest choice is not to order that item.
- Menu allergen tags shown in the app (e.g. "contains nuts," "gluten-free option") are provided by the restaurant and reflect their standard recipe; cross-contact during preparation is possible even when an item is tagged allergen-free.
- If a customer experiences an allergic reaction after eating a delivered order, this is a safety-critical issue and must be escalated to a human agent immediately per Section 13 — it is never something the chatbot resolves with a refund alone.
- Temperature-sensitive items (frozen, must-be-hot, etc.) are packaged by the restaurant to survive typical delivery times; if an item arrives at an unsafe temperature, report it as a food-quality issue under Section 5 unless it has caused illness, in which case it is a safety escalation.

## 9. Tipping

- Tipping is optional and 100% of the tip goes to the courier.
- Tips can be added at checkout or increased for up to 24 hours after delivery.
- Tips are not refunded when an order issue is unrelated to courier service (e.g., wrong item from the restaurant).
- Tips cannot be decreased or removed after the order is delivered, to protect couriers from after-the-fact tip reduction once the delivery is already complete.
- Cash tips, where accepted by local law and the courier, are between the customer and courier directly and are not tracked or refunded through the app.

## 10. Customer Conduct

Prohibited behavior includes, but is not limited to:
- Abusive, threatening, or harassing language toward couriers, restaurant staff, or support agents.
- Falsely reporting missing/wrong/damaged orders to obtain refunds.
- Sharing account credentials or using automated tools (bots) to place orders or claim promotions.
- Fraudulent chargebacks on orders that were successfully delivered.
- Discriminatory remarks or behavior toward couriers or restaurant staff based on protected characteristics.
- Requesting that a courier violate traffic or safety laws (e.g. asking them to speed, ignore delivery zone restrictions, or hand off in an unsafe location) to save time.

Violations may result in warnings, refund restrictions, temporary suspension, or permanent account termination, at Foodly's discretion. Serious violations (harassment, threats, discrimination) may be escalated directly to account termination without a prior warning, and can also trigger the safety-escalation handling described in Section 13 when a courier or restaurant staff member's safety is involved.

## 11. Restaurant Partners

- Restaurants control their own menus, prices, hours, and item availability.
- Restaurants are responsible for order accuracy and food quality; Foodly facilitates ordering, payment, and delivery logistics.
- Customers can rate and review restaurants and couriers after each order; reviews must comply with Foodly's community guidelines (no harassment, hate speech, or personal information).
- A restaurant temporarily going "Unavailable" mid-order-window (e.g. due to a kitchen issue) triggers an automatic cancellation and full refund for any order not yet accepted; already-accepted orders proceed normally.
- Reporting a restaurant for a serious violation (health code concerns, discriminatory treatment, etc.) goes through the in-app "Report a restaurant" flow and is reviewed by Foodly's partner-quality team, separate from a routine order-issue refund request.

## 12. Privacy (Summary)

- Foodly collects the data necessary to fulfill orders: name, delivery address, contact info, payment details, and order history.
- Location data is used only during active deliveries for tracking and courier matching.
- Foodly does not sell personal data to third parties. Data may be shared with restaurant and courier partners only as needed to fulfill an order.
- Full details are available in the separate Privacy Policy document; this summary is for quick chatbot reference only and is not a substitute for the legal policy.
- Customers can request a copy of the personal data Foodly holds about them via Settings → Privacy → Download My Data, or by asking support; exports are provided within 30 days.
- Marketing communications (push notifications, email, SMS) are opt-in and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Notifications without affecting the ability to place orders or receive order-status updates.

## 13. Contact & Escalation

- In-app chat and this chatbot handle most order issues (refunds, delivery status, account questions) instantly.
- Issues the chatbot cannot resolve (safety incidents, legal requests, payment disputes beyond standard refund rules) should be escalated to a human support agent.
- Safety-critical issues (e.g., courier misconduct, food tampering, allergic reaction) must always be escalated immediately, never resolved by automated refund alone.
- Other examples of safety-critical topics that must always reach a human agent rather than being resolved automatically: a courier behaving in a threatening, unsafe, or discriminatory way; visible tampering with packaging or food; a customer or household member becoming ill after eating a delivered order; suspected fraud on the account beyond a simple duplicate charge; and any request involving law enforcement, a subpoena, or a legal claim against Foodly, a restaurant, or a courier.
- Human agents are reachable through the "Talk to a person" option inside the same chat surface, or at support@foodly.com, and are available for safety-critical issues at all hours regardless of local support-hours coverage described in Section 20.

## 14. Group Orders & Split Payments

- A group order lets multiple people add items to one shared cart from a single restaurant, with one delivery to one address. The host starts the group order and shares an in-app link or code with participants.
- Each participant can pay for their own items individually at checkout, or the host can choose to cover the full bill; this choice is made by the host when starting the group order and can't be changed once checkout begins.
- The restaurant's normal minimum order value applies to the group order's combined total, not to each participant's individual contribution.
- Cancellation of a group order after the host has submitted it follows the same stages as a normal order (Section 4); once submitted, individual participants cannot cancel just their own portion.
- Refund requests on a group order (Section 5) are filed by the host, since the host is the order's account of record; the host is responsible for coordinating any reimbursement among participants who paid individually.

## 15. Business & Catering Accounts

- Foodly for Business accounts let a company set up centralized billing, spending limits per employee, and expense reporting exports for individual meal or team-catering orders.
- Catering orders (large orders intended for groups, typically above a restaurant-set item or dollar threshold) often require advance notice — commonly 24–48 hours — since they involve special preparation; the required lead time is shown on a restaurant's catering menu before checkout.
- Business account invoicing (pay-by-invoice instead of a card on file) is available to approved companies after a short verification process; standard consumer accounts remain card- or wallet-funded only.
- Cancellation and refund rules for catering orders follow Sections 4 and 5, except that the "preparation has started" cutoff for catering is set relative to the required advance-notice window rather than a few minutes after acceptance, since catering preparation begins well before the delivery time.
- A business account is still bound by the same customer conduct standards in Section 10; misuse by an individual employee can result in that person's access being restricted without affecting the rest of the company account.

## 16. Foodly Plus Membership

- Foodly Plus is an optional paid subscription that includes reduced or waived delivery fees on eligible orders, occasional member-only promotions, and priority customer support routing.
- Membership renews automatically at the interval and price shown at signup (typically monthly or annually) until canceled; canceling stops future renewal but does not refund the current billing period, except where required by local law.
- A free trial, when offered, converts automatically into a paid membership at the end of the trial unless canceled before the trial ends; the exact trial length and price are always shown before the customer confirms signup.
- Delivery fee savings apply only to orders that meet the eligibility shown in the app (e.g. above a minimum order value, within standard delivery radius); surge pricing and service fees are not waived by membership.
- Membership benefits are tied to the individual account and cannot be shared, transferred, or pooled with other accounts, including within a single household.

## 17. International & Multi-City Service

- Foodly operates in multiple countries and cities; available restaurants, delivery radius, payment methods, and promotions are localized to the customer's current delivery address, not their home city or country.
- Prices are always shown and charged in the local currency of the delivery address's city; currency conversion, if the customer's card is issued in a different currency, is handled by the card issuer, not Foodly.
- Traveling to a new city does not require a new account — the same login works everywhere Foodly operates, and order history is preserved across cities.
- Local regulations (e.g. alcohol sales hours, delivery zone restrictions, tipping norms) vary by city and are reflected automatically in what a customer can order and how checkout behaves in that location.
- Customer support is available in the primary language(s) of each market Foodly operates in; the chatbot defaults to the language the app is set to, and can be switched to English at any time if a human agent in the local language isn't immediately available.

## 18. Accessibility

- The Foodly app is built to work with standard mobile screen readers (VoiceOver, TalkBack) and supports platform-level text-size and high-contrast display settings.
- Delivery instructions can include accessibility notes (e.g. "please knock, doorbell is broken" or "hand order directly to me, no stairs access") saved to an address profile so couriers see them before arrival.
- Customers who are unable to use the in-app chat or chatbot due to a disability can reach a human support agent directly by phone where phone support is available in their market, or by email at support@foodly.com.
- Accessibility-related feedback about the app or about a specific restaurant's physical accessibility can be submitted through Settings → Help → Send Feedback and is routed to the relevant product or partner-quality team.

## 19. Rewards & Loyalty Points

- Eligible orders earn loyalty points at a rate shown in the Rewards tab of the app; points accumulate per account and do not expire as long as the account remains active (see the inactivity handling in Section 6).
- Points can be redeemed for Foodly account credit or select in-app rewards (e.g. a free delivery voucher); redemption is done manually by the customer and points are not auto-applied to orders.
- Points earned on an order are reversed if that order is later fully refunded (for example, under the Section 5 "order never arrived" resolution), since no qualifying purchase actually completed.
- Loyalty status tiers, where offered, are based on rolling 12-month spend or order count and are recalculated periodically; moving to a lower tier does not forfeit already-redeemed rewards.
- Referral rewards (Section 7) and loyalty points are tracked separately and can both be earned on the same qualifying order.

## 20. Support Channels & Response Times

- The in-app chatbot is available 24/7 for order-status questions, refund requests, account questions, and other topics covered in this document.
- "Talk to a person" routes to a human agent; typical first-response time is a few minutes for active-order issues and up to a few hours for general account questions, though safety-critical issues (Section 13) are prioritized immediately regardless of queue volume.
- Email support at support@foodly.com is available for non-urgent issues and issues better handled with attachments (e.g. a photo of a damaged item); typical response time is within one business day.
- Phone support is available in select markets during local business hours; where unavailable, in-app chat and email remain the primary channels.
- Support interactions, including chatbot conversations, may be logged for quality and training purposes, consistent with the data handling described in Section 12.

## 21. Substitutions & Out-of-Stock Items

- If an ordered item becomes unavailable after the order is placed, the restaurant may offer a substitution; where the app supports it, the customer is asked to approve or decline the substitution before it's prepared.
- If a substitution is prepared without customer approval (e.g. due to a technical issue) and the customer doesn't want it, this is treated as a wrong-item issue under Section 5 and is eligible for a refund or credit.
- Price differences from an approved substitution are adjusted automatically — a cheaper substitute is refunded the difference, and a pricier one is not charged extra unless the customer explicitly agreed to the new price.

## 22. Gift Cards

- Foodly gift cards can be purchased in-app or through select retail partners and redeemed as account credit toward any order, subject to the same restaurant and delivery-area availability as any other order.
- Gift card balances do not expire and are not redeemable for cash, except where required by local law.
- A lost or stolen gift card code can be deactivated and, where proof of purchase is available, reissued by support; unredeemed balances on a deleted account (Section 6) are forfeited unless support is contacted before deletion completes.

## 23. Service Disruptions & Force Majeure

- Severe weather, natural disasters, citywide emergencies, or public health orders may cause Foodly to pause delivery in an affected area entirely; affected customers are notified in-app and any pending orders are automatically canceled and refunded in full.
- Delays caused by extreme weather, traffic incidents, or road closures beyond a courier's control are not treated as a courier service failure for tipping purposes (Section 9), though they remain eligible for the standard late-order refund review in Section 5.
- Foodly does not guarantee service continuity during force majeure events and is not liable for losses beyond the value of the specific affected order.

## Quick-Answer FAQ

**Q: My order hasn't arrived and it's past the estimated time. What do I do?**
A: Check the live tracking screen first. If the status hasn't updated in 15+ minutes past the estimate, you're eligible to cancel for a full refund or continue waiting — your choice.

**Q: I received someone else's order / the wrong order.**
A: You're eligible for a full refund or a replacement, reported within 48 hours of delivery.

**Q: Can I change my order after placing it?**
A: Only before the restaurant starts preparing it. After that, the order must be canceled (if eligible) and re-placed.

**Q: How long do refunds take?**
A: Refunds are approved instantly in most cases; funds typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days.

**Q: Do you refund delivery fees and tips?**
A: Only if the order was never delivered, or in cases of a severe service failure reviewed by support.

**Q: Can I cancel after the courier has picked up my order?**
A: No. At that point, contact support about the delivery itself rather than canceling.

**Q: I have a food allergy. Is it safe to order?**
A: Foodly cannot guarantee any item is allergen-free. Contact the restaurant directly before ordering if you have a severe allergy.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Settings → Privacy → Delete Account, or ask support. Processed within 30 days.

**Q: My promo code isn't working.**
A: Check it hasn't expired, meets the minimum order value, and is valid for the restaurant you're ordering from. Promo codes are single-use per account unless stated otherwise.

**Q: A courier was rude or unsafe.**
A: This is always escalated to a human agent immediately — do not resolve with a refund alone.

**Q: Can I split the bill on a group order?**
A: Yes — each participant can pay for their own items individually, or the host can cover the whole order, depending on what the host chooses when starting the group order.

**Q: Does my company need a special account for team lunches?**
A: A Foodly for Business account is optional but useful for centralized billing and expense reporting; regular personal accounts can still place catering or group orders.

**Q: What happens to my Foodly Plus membership if I cancel mid-cycle?**
A: You keep the benefits until the end of the current billing period; it just won't renew after that, and the current period isn't refunded except where local law requires it.

**Q: I'm traveling to another city — do I need a new account?**
A: No, the same account works everywhere Foodly operates; restaurants and pricing just update to match your delivery address.

**Q: Do my loyalty points expire?**
A: No, as long as the account stays active. Points earned on an order are removed if that specific order is later fully refunded.

**Q: How fast will a human agent respond?**
A: A few minutes for active-order issues, up to a few hours for general questions — and immediately for safety-critical issues like courier misconduct or a reaction to food.

**Q: An item I ordered was out of stock and got swapped for something else.**
A: If you approved the substitution, that's expected; if it was swapped without your approval and you don't want it, that's treated as a wrong-item issue and is refund-eligible.

**Q: Does my gift card balance expire?**
A: No, gift card balances don't expire and aren't redeemable for cash except where local law requires it.

**Q: There's a storm and my order was canceled automatically — will I be charged?**
A: No. Orders automatically canceled due to a service-wide disruption like severe weather are fully refunded.
