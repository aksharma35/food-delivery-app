# Foodly Customer Policies

_Last updated: 2026-08-26_

This document is the knowledge base for Foodly's customer support chatbot. It covers ordering, delivery, payments, cancellations, refunds, accounts, promotions, and conduct. Each section is self-contained so it can be chunked, embedded, or quoted directly by a chatbot without losing context. A quick-answer FAQ is included at the end for direct Q&A grounding.

---

## 1. Ordering

- Customers can order from any restaurant partner shown as **"Open"** in the app. Restaurants marked "Closed" or "Unavailable" cannot be ordered from.
- An order is confirmed once the restaurant accepts it, typically within 2 minutes of submission. Orders not accepted within 5 minutes are automatically canceled and refunded in full.
- Minimum order values, if any, are set per restaurant and shown before checkout.
- Customers may add special instructions (e.g. "no onions," "leave at door") in the notes field. Restaurants and couriers make reasonable efforts to honor them but cannot guarantee compliance for allergy-critical requests — see Section 8 (Food Safety & Allergies).
- Once a restaurant begins preparing an order, it can no longer be modified. To change an order, cancel it (if eligible, see Section 4) and place a new one.

## 2. Pricing & Payments

- Menu prices are set by the restaurant and may differ slightly from in-store prices.
- Total cost includes: item subtotal, delivery fee, service fee, applicable taxes, and optional tip.
- Accepted payment methods: credit/debit cards, digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and Foodly account credit. Cash on delivery is available only in select cities and is marked at checkout.
- Payment is authorized at checkout and charged once the order is confirmed by the restaurant. If an order is canceled before confirmation, the authorization is released (funds typically reappear within 3–5 business days, timing depends on the customer's bank).
- Prices, fees, and delivery ranges can change based on demand (surge pricing) during peak times; the final price is always shown before payment is confirmed.

## 3. Delivery

- Delivery availability and estimated time are shown before checkout and are based on courier availability, distance, and restaurant prep time.
- Average delivery time is 25–40 minutes; this is an estimate, not a guarantee.
- Customers can track their order in real time from the "Order Status" screen, including courier location once the order is picked up.
- Couriers will attempt delivery at the address provided. If a customer is unreachable for more than 10 minutes after the courier arrives, the order may be marked as delivered or returned, at the courier's discretion, without a refund.
- Contactless delivery ("leave at door") can be selected at checkout for eligible orders.
- Delivery areas are limited to each restaurant's active radius, shown automatically based on the customer's delivery address.

## 4. Cancellations

- **Before restaurant acceptance:** Orders can be canceled free of charge from the app.
- **After restaurant acceptance, before preparation starts:** Orders can usually still be canceled with a full refund; a short grace-period window is shown in the app.
- **After preparation has started:** Orders generally cannot be canceled, since the restaurant has already committed ingredients and labor. Refunds at this stage are handled case-by-case (see Section 5).
- **After the order has been picked up by a courier:** Cancellation is not possible. Contact support if there is an issue with the delivery itself.

## 5. Refunds & Order Issues

Foodly issues refunds or credits for the following, upon request within **48 hours** of delivery:

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Order never arrived | Full refund |
| Missing item(s) | Refund or credit for missing item(s) |
| Wrong item(s) delivered | Refund or credit for incorrect item(s) |
| Item(s) arrived damaged or spilled | Refund or credit for affected item(s) |
| Order significantly late (customer no longer wants it) | Reviewed case-by-case; may qualify for partial/full refund |
| Poor food quality (cold, undercooked, etc.) | Reviewed case-by-case; may qualify for credit |

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method or as Foodly account credit, customer's choice where available.
- Refund processing takes 5–10 business days to appear on a statement, depending on the bank.
- Repeated refund requests on an account may trigger manual review to prevent abuse.
- Delivery fees and tips are refunded only when the order was not delivered at all, or at support's discretion for severe service failures.

## 6. Accounts

- One Foodly account per person; accounts are non-transferable.
- Customers must be 18+ to create an account and place orders (age verification may be required for alcohol purchases).
- Account email and phone number can be updated in Settings. Payment methods can be added/removed at any time.
- Accounts inactive for 24+ months may have saved payment methods removed for security; the account itself is not deleted.
- Customers can request full account deletion and data removal at any time via Settings → Privacy → Delete Account, or by contacting support. This is processed within 30 days.

## 7. Promotions, Discounts & Referrals

- Promo codes are single-use per account unless stated otherwise, cannot be combined unless explicitly allowed, and may have minimum order values or restaurant restrictions.
- Foodly reserves the right to cancel a promotion, or revoke a discount retroactively, in cases of suspected fraud or abuse (e.g., creating multiple accounts to claim a first-order discount repeatedly).
- Referral rewards are credited only after the referred user completes their first qualifying order.
- Free delivery subscriptions (if offered) renew automatically until canceled and are billed per the plan's stated terms shown at signup.

## 8. Food Safety & Allergies

- Foodly is a delivery platform; food is prepared by independent restaurant partners, who are responsible for ingredient accuracy and safe food handling.
- Customers with severe allergies should contact the restaurant directly (via in-app chat, where available) before ordering and should not rely solely on menu descriptions or special-instruction notes.
- Foodly cannot guarantee any dish is allergen-free, even if requested. When in doubt, the safest choice is not to order that item.

## 9. Tipping

- Tipping is optional and 100% of the tip goes to the courier.
- Tips can be added at checkout or increased for up to 24 hours after delivery.
- Tips are not refunded when an order issue is unrelated to courier service (e.g., wrong item from the restaurant).

## 10. Customer Conduct

Prohibited behavior includes, but is not limited to:
- Abusive, threatening, or harassing language toward couriers, restaurant staff, or support agents.
- Falsely reporting missing/wrong/damaged orders to obtain refunds.
- Sharing account credentials or using automated tools (bots) to place orders or claim promotions.
- Fraudulent chargebacks on orders that were successfully delivered.

Violations may result in warnings, refund restrictions, temporary suspension, or permanent account termination, at Foodly's discretion.

## 11. Restaurant Partners

- Restaurants control their own menus, prices, hours, and item availability.
- Restaurants are responsible for order accuracy and food quality; Foodly facilitates ordering, payment, and delivery logistics.
- Customers can rate and review restaurants and couriers after each order; reviews must comply with Foodly's community guidelines (no harassment, hate speech, or personal information).

## 12. Privacy (Summary)

- Foodly collects the data necessary to fulfill orders: name, delivery address, contact info, payment details, and order history.
- Location data is used only during active deliveries for tracking and courier matching.
- Foodly does not sell personal data to third parties. Data may be shared with restaurant and courier partners only as needed to fulfill an order.
- Full details are available in the separate Privacy Policy document; this summary is for quick chatbot reference only and is not a substitute for the legal policy.

## 13. Contact & Escalation

- In-app chat and this chatbot handle most order issues (refunds, delivery status, account questions) instantly.
- Issues the chatbot cannot resolve (safety incidents, legal requests, payment disputes beyond standard refund rules) should be escalated to a human support agent.
- Safety-critical issues (e.g., courier misconduct, food tampering, allergic reaction) must always be escalated immediately, never resolved by automated refund alone.

## Quick-Answer FAQ

**Q: My order hasn't arrived and it's past the estimated time. What do I do?**
A: Check the live tracking screen first. If the status hasn't updated in 15+ minutes past the estimate, you're eligible to cancel for a full refund or continue waiting — your choice.

**Q: I received someone else's order / the wrong order.**
A: You're eligible for a full refund or a replacement, reported within 48 hours of delivery.

**Q: Can I change my order after placing it?**
A: Only before the restaurant starts preparing it. After that, the order must be canceled (if eligible) and re-placed.

**Q: How long do refunds take?**
A: Refunds are approved instantly in most cases; funds typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days.

**Q: Do you refund delivery fees and tips?**
A: Only if the order was never delivered, or in cases of a severe service failure reviewed by support.

**Q: Can I cancel after the courier has picked up my order?**
A: No. At that point, contact support about the delivery itself rather than canceling.

**Q: I have a food allergy. Is it safe to order?**
A: Foodly cannot guarantee any item is allergen-free. Contact the restaurant directly before ordering if you have a severe allergy.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Settings → Privacy → Delete Account, or ask support. Processed within 30 days.

**Q: My promo code isn't working.**
A: Check it hasn't expired, meets the minimum order value, and is valid for the restaurant you're ordering from. Promo codes are single-use per account unless stated otherwise.

**Q: A courier was rude or unsafe.**
A: This is always escalated to a human agent immediately — do not resolve with a refund alone.
